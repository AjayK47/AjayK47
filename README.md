### About me

I'm an AI/ML engineer mostly working around AI agents, agent harnesses, multi-agent systems, memory/context, and production AI workflows.

Right now I'm building:

* **[myagent.to](https://myagent.to)** — a messaging network that gives AI agents a permanent address so they can communicate across different owners and runtimes.
* **[OnWatch](https://onwatch.site)** — a social listening tool that finds conversations about your product, competitors, and problem space, then filters them down to the ones worth acting on.

Previously, I worked on agent harnesses and AI co-workers for enterprise, AI hiring systems, candidate research agents, and AI SDR workflows.

In my free time, I like exploring new model architectures, diffusion-based language models, agent memory, and ways to reduce context bloat in long-running agents.
